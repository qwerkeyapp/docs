# Qwerkey Support

Qwerkey is a compact, movable iPhone keyboard designed to fit your reach.

## Contact

For help, bug reports, purchase questions, or privacy requests, email
[contact@qwerkey.app](mailto:contact@qwerkey.app).

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of what
happened. Do not include passwords, payment-card information, or sensitive text
you typed.

## Enable Qwerkey

1. Open **Settings** on your iPhone.
2. Choose **General → Keyboard → Keyboards**.
3. Tap **Add New Keyboard**, then choose **Qwerkey**.
4. In a text field, hold the globe key and choose Qwerkey.

Qwerkey does not require **Allow Full Access**. It can remain disabled.

## Customize the keyboard

Use the controls above the keys to adjust the keyboard's position, key size,
shape, appearance, spacing, and typing language. Swipe the controls sideways to
see additional options. The chevron hides or shows the controls, and the eye
button toggles the text preview.

## Purchases and the free trial

Qwerkey includes a seven-day free trial. The trial does not start a subscription
and does not charge you automatically when it ends.

Qwerkey Lifetime is a one-time In-App Purchase. The purchase is handled by Apple;
Qwerkey does not receive or store your payment-card information.

To restore a previous purchase, open the Qwerkey app, open the Qwerkey Lifetime
purchase screen, and tap **Restore Purchase**. Use the same Apple Account that
made the original purchase.

For refunds or billing changes, use Apple's purchase support at
[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/).

## Troubleshooting

### Qwerkey does not appear

Confirm that it is listed under **Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards**.
If it is already listed, remove it, restart the iPhone, and add it again.

Some apps and secure fields, including password fields, may temporarily switch
to Apple's keyboard or prevent third-party keyboards. This is controlled by iOS
or the app containing the text field.

### The keyboard does not use mobile data

Qwerkey works on the device without network access. iOS may therefore omit a
separate mobile-data switch for Qwerkey. Wi-Fi, mobile data, and Full Access are
not required for typing.

### A purchase is not showing

Check that the device is signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase,
then use **Restore Purchase** in the Qwerkey app. If the App Store reports a
billing problem, contact Apple Support. If Apple confirms the purchase but it
still does not unlock, email [contact@qwerkey.app](mailto:contact@qwerkey.app).
