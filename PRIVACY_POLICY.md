# Qwerkey Privacy Policy

Effective: 24 September 2026

Qwerkey does not collect, transmit, sell, or use the text you type. The keyboard operates on the device without network access and does not request Full Access.

## Information stored on the device

Qwerkey stores keyboard preferences, the date the seven-day trial began, and whether Apple has verified a Qwerkey Lifetime purchase. These records remain on the device or in Qwerkey's private shared app container and are used only to operate the app and keyboard.

## Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Qwerkey receives only the StoreKit information required to determine whether the Qwerkey Lifetime purchase is active. Qwerkey does not receive or store payment-card details.

## Typed text

Qwerkey processes key taps and nearby text locally to provide keyboard functions. It does not send typed text to Qwerkey or any third party.

## Network and mobile data

Qwerkey does not use Wi-Fi or mobile data and does not require a network connection. iOS may not show a separate mobile-data switch for Qwerkey because the keyboard does not request network access. Allow Full Access is not required and can remain disabled.

## Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Qwerkey contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and performs no cross-app tracking.

## Retention and deletion

Deleting Qwerkey removes its app data and preferences. A small trial-start record may remain in the iOS Keychain to prevent repeated trials. Purchases remain associated with the user's Apple Account and can be restored through the App Store. Privacy assistance can be requested at [contact@qwerkey.app](mailto:contact@qwerkey.app).

## Changes

If Qwerkey's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the new practices take effect.
